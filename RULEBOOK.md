# Fahrenheit 451: The Fireman’s Choice — Rulebook draft

An original browser adaptation of Ray Bradbury’s novel. Review this rulebook against the final classroom build before submitting.

## Goal
Guide Guy Montag through five chapters and fifteen numbered challenges. Explore each room, follow the objective, and decide how to respond to the people he meets. The main story follows the novel’s event order. At Granger’s fire, choose one of three conclusions. Preservation is closest to the novel; Defiance and Submission are labeled alternate adaptations. The city’s destruction occurs in every conclusion.

There are no points for violence, no combat system, and no lives to run out of. A missed encounter returns you to a nearby checkpoint or repeats that question.

## Start
Open the GitHub Pages game in Chrome or Safari. Choose **New Game** to begin. If a valid checkpoint exists, **Continue** resumes it; New Game asks before replacing it. The game runs without an account or installation. Use landscape orientation on a phone.

| Action | Keyboard | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A/D or left/right arrows | Hold left/right buttons |
| Interact | E, Enter, or Space | ACT |
| Fill an interaction meter | Hold an interaction key | Hold ACT |
| Finish a line / advance dialogue | Press interaction key again | Tap the dialogue box |
| Select a response | Up/down, then interaction key | Tap its full row |
| Dive in the river | Hold Down or S | Hold DIVE; move with a second finger |
| Journal | J | JOURNAL |
| Pause | Esc or P | II |
| Resume | Esc or choose Resume | Tap Resume |

Keyboard auto-repeat does not advance dialogue. Release and press again. Tap once to reveal a line immediately, then again to continue. There is no timed reading requirement.

## Challenges

| Chapter | Challenges | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 The Burn; 02 Clarisse’s Question; 03 Parlor Walls | Soak three piles; ignite them; walk with Clarisse; inspect the walls, talk to Mildred three times, and read her script. |
| 2 | 04 The Woman’s House; 05 Beatty’s Argument; 06 The Hidden Books | Hide the falling book when Beatty looks away; answer his visit; move the chair, hold at the vent, and stay still at the door. |
| 3 | 07 Find Faber; 08 The Sieve and the Sand; 09 Dover Beach | Find the file-wallet before using the phone; recover the verse; speak with Faber; switch off the walls, read, and burn the poem. |
| 4 | 10 Montag’s House; 11 The Boulevard; 12 The Count of Ten | Burn the beds and three walls; respond to the Hound prompt; cross between passing headlights; mask your scent and reach the river. |
| 5 | 13 The River; 14 The Book People; 15 The Fireman’s Choice | Dive under searchlights; find the shore and rail; recall the passage with Granger; choose and view an ending. |

Glowing objectives and the current prompt show nearby actions. A locked object explains its prerequisite. “WATCHING” means wait for Beatty to turn away. Headlight lanes have safe gaps; wait outside a lit lane before crossing. Moving during the stillness encounter restarts that wait. Incorrect recall answers repeat without erasing earlier chapters.

## Saving and finishing
Normal play saves after a complete story event, hold action, or room transition. Reloading during an unfinished conversation returns to the preceding checkpoint. If storage is blocked, play continues but a save warning appears; do not rely on Continue. Pause → Save & quit returns to the title. After an ending, tap or press E to return; Continue can show that completed ending again.

The journal lists all fifteen challenges and derives completion from actual game flags. A normal run must complete all fifteen.

## Presentation Mode
Choose any chapter for a short classroom demonstration. Earlier flags are supplied as demonstration defaults, clearly labeled, and never written to the normal save. They are not proof that those challenges were earned. Quit to title and choose another chapter as needed.
