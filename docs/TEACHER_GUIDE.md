# Fahrenheit 451: The Fireman's Choice — teacher one-page guide

**Link:** https://hewwobuddy2023-dev.github.io/fahrenheit451-game/

> **Status, Nov 13:** the link above still serves the **older** published build. The final-sprint build goes live only after the student approves publishing it. The link has **not yet been tested on a school Chromebook** behind the school filter. The student will check it on Monday and update this line.

## Before class (5 minutes)
1. Open the link on one school Chromebook and press **New Game**. The first scene should appear within a few seconds.
2. Headphones are recommended. Sound starts after the first key press or tap; **M** mutes it at any time. Every danger also has an on-screen text warning, so the game is playable muted.
3. Optional: put the link on the board as a QR code.

## Controls (Chromebook)
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | A / D or arrow keys |
| Run | Hold Shift |
| Interact / talk / continue | E (Enter and Space also work) |
| Hold to act (soak the books, lift the grill) | Hold E; the meter shows progress |
| Choose an answer | Up / Down, then E |
| Vault a low fence | Space at the prompt |
| Dive in the river | Hold S or Down |
| Journal / pause / hint | J / Esc / H |

Touch screens show on-screen buttons automatically. Settings → Controls can force Keyboard or Touch.

## Length and saving
- **Expected length: not yet measured.** A content-based estimate is about 38 minutes of active play for a first-time player (reading about 24 of those), plus time for mistakes, the journal and the other endings. No student has been timed yet; the two real playtests will give the actual numbers.
- Progress saves automatically at each checkpoint on that Chromebook and browser. **Continue** resumes it.
- Students who finish early can press **R** on the ending card to explore the other two endings. That replay never overwrites their save.

## If something goes wrong
- **Stuck in a scene:** Esc → Save & quit → Continue. This reloads the last checkpoint.
- **No sound:** press any key once, then check M (mute) and the Chromebook volume.
- **Page won't load (filter or no internet):** the student can run the game from a laptop with a local copy (see `DEPLOYMENT_GITHUB_PAGES.md`), or demo it on the projector using **Presentation Mode** (title menu), which jumps to any chapter without saving.

## Privacy and accessibility
- No accounts, logins, analytics, microphone or camera. Nothing leaves the browser except the page download.
- Settings include Reduced motion (removes shake, flash and grain), volume, Assisted hose (no pressure management) and Analog effects off.
- Text warnings never rely on colour alone.

## About the story
- The game follows the novel's three parts across five playable chapters.
- The **Preservation** ending is labelled as the one closest to the novel. **Defiance** and **Submission** are labelled as invented "what if" endings.
