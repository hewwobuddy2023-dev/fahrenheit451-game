# Phoenix visual standard

Detailed stylized retro-futurist America; theatrical side-on staging with consistent 392px back-floor seam and 480px walk line. Practical lamp pools, dimensional door returns, architectural ledges, wire runs, curved furniture, weathered surfaces and book spines replace undecorated slabs. Charcoal/cream remain, with industrial olive, television cyan, cold moonlight and natural river/camp color. Interaction targets stay at the same world coordinates and never attach to parallax decoration.

The opening is confident and ordered; middle chapters introduce diegetic broadcast faces/tracking, late city scenes emphasize surveillance, forest uses spacious quiet composition. Strong interference lives inside screens rather than across text. Existing reduced-motion preference also suppresses optional analog motion; no rapid flashing. No new full-screen shader passes by default. Art fixtures and actual browser screenshots are labeled separately.

A generated layer is acceptable only after inspection, compression, perspective/alignment checks and a working procedural fallback. Do not approve a whole campaign from one attractive illustration. Compare all eleven room fixtures plus native mobile views.
