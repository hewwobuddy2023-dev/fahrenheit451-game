## Acceptable sources

Original assets created specifically for the project; public-domain or clearly licensed assets with attribution when required; source documentation from official sites. Review each asset's license. Do not assume an image generated by a general web search is free to use. Do not copy copyrighted game screenshots into the distributed build.

The novel's text is not a shipped asset.

## Original procedural art

`src/art/fx.ts`: eight-frame flame atlas and wake texture. `src/art/figures.ts`: character silhouettes and poses. `src/art/kits.ts`, `src/art/set.ts` and `src/world/stage.ts`: room kits, set dressing, trees, distant forest and ground planes. All painted at runtime by project code; no downloaded artwork.

`src/assets/city-backdrop.webp` (1536x512, about 44 KB): original artwork for this project, used as a distant city layer on the title screen and in city rooms. It does not affect interaction and is omitted from the forest bombing horizon.

## Original audio

`src/audio.ts` synthesizes every sound locally with Web Audio: a seeded two-second mono noise buffer, a sine room tone and short enveloped oscillator cues. No recorded music, samples or runtime audio requests. At most two continuous sources and eight short cues play at once.
