## Primary characters

- **Montag:** main character; transitions from dutiful fireman to questioning and preserving knowledge. Player decisions vary his emotional response, not the historical fact that book burning is oppressive.
- **Clarisse:** inquisitive, perceptive; sparks Montag's self-examination, not a late-game quest giver.
- **Mildred:** parlor screen attachment and emotional isolation; avoid caricature or turning overdose into reward-oriented minigame.
- **Beatty:** intelligent, articulate advocate of conformity; arguments must have substance rather than cartoon-villain lines.
- **Faber:** values content, quality of information, and reflection; guides ideas rather than simply handing out items.
- **Mechanical Hound:** oppressive surveillance and physical threat, implemented as a deterministic encounter to protect schedule/performance.
- **Granger/book people:** memorize writings for preservation rather than hoarding physical books.
