# Three-to-five-minute presentation — speaking draft

Use your own phrasing. Rehearse it against `DEMO_ROUTE.md`; the route’s timings have not been measured with a human presenter.

**Title and purpose**

“My project is *Fahrenheit 451: The Fireman’s Choice*, based on Ray Bradbury’s novel. It has five chapters and fifteen challenges connected to characters and events in the story. You move through rooms, interact with objects, and choose responses. I’m using Presentation Mode to show a short route. It skips to chapters without changing a normal saved game.”

**Opening burn and choice**

“The opening has Montag enjoying his work as a fireman. I kept that because he does not start the book already understanding everything that is wrong with his society. Here, I hold the interaction key to soak the book piles. Then I return to Beatty and choose a response. The action is part of Montag’s ordinary routine before Clarisse makes him question it.”

[Demonstrate a hold action and the ignition response. Do not say the choice changes the novel’s whole plot.]

**Theme through play**

“My main theme is the loss of independent thought through distraction and conformity. Later, the player has to keep trying to speak to Mildred through the parlor walls. On the subway, a jingle competes with a passage Montag is trying to remember. Those challenges let the player experience distraction instead of only being told that it is a problem.”

**River and preservation**

“I’m jumping to Chapter 5 now. The river escape uses searchlights. On a keyboard I hold Down to dive; on a phone there is a DIVE button. Getting caught gives a local retry. After the river, Granger’s group preserves books by remembering them. The recall challenge connects the player’s action directly to that idea.”

[Show a searchlight crossing, then Granger’s recall.]

**Ending and scope**

“There are three endings. Preservation is closest to the novel. Defiance and Submission are alternate adaptations, and the game labels them that way. The city is destroyed in all three. The player chooses how Montag responds, rather than magically preventing the war. I’m showing Preservation.”

[Choose the ending; advance through its story.]

“The journal lists all fifteen challenges. In a normal game they must be completed in order. Presentation Mode supplies earlier progress for demonstrations, so that is not evidence of a full playthrough. My rulebook explains the controls and retry rules. My connection statement will use three quotations checked against the same printed edition.”
